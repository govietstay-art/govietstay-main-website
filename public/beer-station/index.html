Is Tropical Chill Station open yet?

Not yet confirmed. This is a visual brand and landing-page concept. Opening date, operating hours and exact location will be announced once finalized.

Where is Sơn Trà Night Market?

The popular Sơn Trà Night Market is in Da Nang, near the east side of Dragon Bridge. Use the map link above for the market; it is not a confirmed pin for our beer station.

Can I watch the Dragon Bridge fire show from the station?

The artwork celebrates Dragon Bridge. Direct visibility from the planned station needs an on-site sightline check, and the public show schedule may change.

Can I see the beer menu or book a table?

The menu, prices, booking details and opening information are still being finalized. This preview is intended to show the visual direction of the brand.
