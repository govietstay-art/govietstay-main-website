Three ways the kit could feel.

These pages are not wired into the Travel Kit. PR #80 is unchanged. Each concept is a static page with its own layout. The logo goes to the main site. Nothing here is a tour, a price, or a promised reply time.

A · Cinematic The photograph is the wall. One italic line. Three stills of Da Nang, not a card grid. B · Companion A real clock, one decision, four situations. Russian is the default, so the long lines have to fit. C · Concierge A letter and a tray of requests. WhatsApp opens only when you tap. No invented face.

Recommendation, not a decision

Do not ship A, B, or C whole. The brand line is trusted local support, so C is the right spine: a note, a tray, you press send, no fake person, no promised speed. Give that spine A’s full-bleed photograph for the arrival moment, and B’s single “what now” line plus the real clock. The card grid on PR #80 would be retired, not recoloured.

The owner chooses. Nothing here is integrated until then.
