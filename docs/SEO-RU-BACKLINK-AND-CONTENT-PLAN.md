# GoVietStay — Russian SEO authority plan

Updated: 20 August 2026

Primary audience: Russian-speaking travelers from Russia and Kazakhstan

Primary market: Vietnam, especially Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue and Phu Quoc

## Goal

Build real Russian-language search authority around travel planning, excursions,
airport transfers and entry preparation. The objective is not a high backlink
count. The objective is a small number of relevant mentions that send qualified
travelers to useful pages.

## Rules

- Publish for people first. Every post must answer a real traveler question.
- Do not buy bulk links, use automated directory submissions or repeat the same
  exact-match anchor across many sites.
- Link to the page that best answers the question, not always to the homepage.
- Disclose partnerships where required.
- Keep visa and weather content dated and link to official sources.
- Ask partners to keep links crawlable, but accept `nofollow` where a platform
  applies it automatically. Referral traffic still has value.
- Record every live mention, publication date and destination URL in the tracker.

## Publication checklist

Before each new article goes live:

1. One primary traveler question appears naturally in the title and H1.
2. The first paragraph gives the short answer.
3. At least one first-hand local detail, operational check or original photo is
   included.
4. Prices, weather, entry rules and operating status are dated.
5. Relevant official or primary sources are linked.
6. The article links to one city hub, one relevant tour/service page and one
   related article.
7. The title, description, canonical, breadcrumb and article schema are present.
8. The page is added to the root sitemap and requested in Search Console only
   after production verification.

## Weekly success metrics

Record every Friday:

- Russian-page impressions and clicks in Search Console.
- Queries containing Дананг, Хойан, Хюэ, Фукуок, трансфер and виза.
- Impressions and clicks from Russia and Kazakhstan.
- Number of referring domains and live relevant mentions.
- WhatsApp conversations that name Google, Telegram, VK, Dzen or a partner.
- Pages with impressions but low CTR, for title/description improvement.
- Pages with no impressions after four to six weeks, for content and internal
  link review rather than repeated indexing requests.

## What not to do

- Do not submit the site to hundreds of generic directories.
- Do not pay for sitewide footer links or hidden links.
- Do not publish the same article across many sites with only small changes.
- Do not promise visas, weather, sea conditions or guide availability.
- Do not request indexing repeatedly every day.
- Do not judge the campaign after a few days. New pages need crawling, indexing,
  relevance signals and genuine external mentions before stable search results
  can develop.
