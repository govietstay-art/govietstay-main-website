# GoVietStay Italy Market Playbook
Updated: 2026-08-27

## Target
Italian FIT / fai-da-te travelers, couples, families and small private groups.
Do NOT compete for large package-tour groups.

## Core positioning
Volo e hotel li scegli tu. In Vietnam hai un team locale quando serve davvero.

## Pricing rule
STANDARD TOUR PRICE = SAME AS ENGLISH/PUBLIC GOVIETSTAY PRICE.
No Italy surcharge.

Standard anchors:
- bana: 1,550,000 VND ≈ EUR 50.9
- cham: 950,000 VND ≈ EUR 31.2
- hoian: 1,250,000 VND ≈ EUR 41.1
- hue: 1,450,000 VND ≈ EUR 47.6
- sontra: 850,000 VND ≈ EUR 27.9
- pq3: 1,040,000 VND ≈ EUR 34.2
- pq4: 1,690,000 VND ≈ EUR 55.5

EUR is only a convenience estimate; VND is booking currency.

## Private
Private is the hero product.
Quote by:
- number of guests
- vehicle
- requested guide language
- duration
- tickets
- special requests

## Guide language
Italian or any requested language can be sourced subject to availability and confirmation.
Never promise a language before checking the date.

## WIIFM
Lead with:
- less waiting
- no strangers in confirmed private components
- later/more human departure times
- more time at the places the guest likes
- no useless shopping stops
- family/senior pace
- direct local support when weather/flight/sea conditions change

## SEO
All /it slugs are intentionally in Italian from day one.
Locale: it-IT.

## UX
- mobile body 16-17px
- H1 mobile 36-38px
- button tap targets >= 46px
- real GoVietStay images
- official GoVietStay logo
- WhatsApp-first
- Google Maps trust link
- no fake reviews
- no fake scarcity
- no automatic payment before final confirmation
