# GoVietStay Taiwan SEO Market Playbook

Market: Taiwan
Language: Traditional Chinese (`zh-TW`)
Hub: `/tw`
Primary search engine: Google
Primary website conversion: WhatsApp / local trip enquiry

## Market rationale
- Taiwan is a meaningful Da Nang source market and is consistently among the city's leading international visitor sources.
- Google has dominant search share in Taiwan, so Google SEO + Search Console is a direct fit.
- Taiwan-facing travel SERPs strongly feature 峴港自由行, 5天4夜, 巴拿山黃金佛手橋, 會安/迦南島簸箕船, 峴港包車, 機場接送 and e-Visa preparation.

## Editorial rules
- Use Taiwan Traditional Chinese terminology, not Simplified Chinese.
- Use 峴港, 會安, 巴拿山, 黃金佛手橋, 迦南島簸箕船, 占婆島.
- Do not claim Chinese-speaking guides unless the specific product confirms it.
- Say travellers may send Traditional Chinese messages; product guide language is separately confirmed.
- Visa information must link to official Vietnam Immigration/e-Visa sources.
- Sea-tour operation must be described as weather/authority dependent.
- Avoid fake reviews, fake ratings and keyword stuffing.

## Technical SEO
- `/tw` hub with TravelAgency + WebSite structured data.
- 10 guides with Article + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList.
- Canonical and `zh-TW` hreflang.
- Hub links to all 11 guides; every guide links to 3 related guides and hub/CTA.
- Add `/tw` + all guides to dynamic sitemap.
- Build before commit/push.
- Never stage local admin files during SEO deployment.

## Next growth layer
- Request indexing manually in Google Search Console when quota is available.
- Publish matching Traditional Chinese social posts, each linking to the exact landing page.
- Outreach to Taiwan travel creators/blogs for relevant links.
- Track landing-page impressions -> clicks -> WhatsApp -> bookings.


## Phu Quoc expansion
Phu Quoc is treated as a separate destination cluster for Taiwan travellers, not as a Da Nang add-on. First page: `/tw/phu-quoc-free-travel`. Expand later into PQC airport transfer, family resorts, island hopping and north/south island pages if Search Console impressions justify it.
