# GoVietStay India SEO Market Playbook

Market: India
Language strategy: English for India (`en-IN`) first, not automatic Hindi translation.
Primary conversion channel: WhatsApp.
Hub: `/in`

## Why this market architecture
Current India-facing holiday platforms visibly segment Vietnam/Da Nang demand into Indian Group Tour, Family, Honeymoon and Budget categories. Central Vietnam packages repeatedly feature Ba Na Hills, Hoi An, airport transfers and visa support.

## Editorial rules
- Answer the search query above the fold.
- Do not pretend vegetarian means strict Jain.
- Never promise visa approval.
- Link visa claims to official Vietnam Immigration / e-Visa sources.
- Do not state an exchange-rate conversion as permanent.
- Prices and operating conditions must be phrased as current/from/subject to confirmation.
- One major attraction per day is the default itinerary logic.
- Always collect dates, guest count, hotel and dietary needs before giving a final plan.

## Technical SEO
- Canonical per page.
- `en-IN` hreflang.
- Article + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage schema for guides.
- TravelAgency + WebSite schema on India section.
- Hub -> 10 guides.
- Every guide -> 3 related guides + hub/local CTA.
- Sitemap generated from shared data source.
- Build must pass before commit/push.
- Never commit local admin files as part of an SEO deploy.

## Public research anchors used for strategy
- MakeMyTrip Da Nang packages: family, honeymoon, Indian group and budget segmentation.
- Vietnam Immigration / official e-Visa portal for changing visa conditions.
- Vietnam Tourism official e-Visa guidance.

## Next growth layer after indexation
- Google Search Console: submit `/in` + 10 guides.
- Indian travel communities and creator outreach.
- English social posts written for Indian families/couples, linking to the exact relevant guide.
- Build backlinks to the highest-impression guides, not all pages equally.
- Track page -> WhatsApp click -> lead -> booking.
