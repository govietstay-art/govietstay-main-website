# GoVietStay Philippines Market Playbook
Updated: 2026-08-27

## Target
FIT / DIY travelers, couples, families and barkadas.
Do NOT compete for outbound package-tour groups.

## Positioning
Book your flight and hotel yourself. We handle the part that gets complicated once you arrive.

## Market behavior
- Strong comfort with OTAs such as Klook / Traveloka / Booking.
- Social discovery is important; Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Instagram matter.
- Direct Manila–Da Nang air connectivity lowers friction.
- Ordinary Philippine passport currently has a 21-day Vietnam visa exemption under the official Vietnam list.

## What we sell
- airport transfers as traffic products
- Ba Na / Hoi An / Cham / Hue local activities
- private cars
- private family/couple/barkada days
- last-day check-out + Hoi An + airport routing
- Phu Quoc island products + private transport

## Pricing approach
VND is the official booking currency.
PHP is a display estimate only.
Launch from-rates:
- bana: 1,490,000 VND ≈ PHP 3,528
- hoian: 1,090,000 VND ≈ PHP 2,581
- cham: 950,000 VND ≈ PHP 2,249
- hue: 1,390,000 VND ≈ PHP 3,291
- dadTransfer: 250,000 VND ≈ PHP 592
- pq3: 699,000 VND ≈ PHP 1,655
- pq4: 1,490,000 VND ≈ PHP 3,528

## Benchmark logic
Current Klook Philippines pages show:
- Hoi An + Coconut Forest private day around 1.189M VND.
- Phu Quoc 3-island entry tours around USD 26.70.
- Phu Quoc 4-island + cable around USD 60.
Use these only as benchmark signals; inclusions and dates differ.
