# GoVietStay China Mainland Market Playbook
Updated: 2026-08-26

## Core positioning
机票酒店你自己选，落地越南以后，我们在当地帮你。
Buy flights/hotels wherever convenient in China. GoVietStay owns the local layer after arrival.

## Language
- Simplified Chinese
- Locale: zh-CN
- Do NOT copy /tw Traditional Chinese and convert characters mechanically.

1. WeChat: inquiry + retention + referrals

## Payment truth
Vietnam supports Alipay and Weixin Pay at participating VietQRGlobal merchants.
Do not state that GoVietStay directly accepts them until the merchant/payment arrangement is confirmed.

## Phu Quoc
Foreign passport holders meeting the special Phu Quoc policy can stay visa-free up to 30 days.
Do not imply that this automatically authorizes travel onward into mainland Vietnam.
