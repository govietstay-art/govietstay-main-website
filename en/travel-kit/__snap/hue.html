Questions people ask first

What does this kit do for me in the first hour?

It tells you the next useful step for the city and the sky: the airport list, a phrase to show, or a roof if it is raining. Then it keeps emergency numbers, a phrasebook, a packing list and a reference exchange rate on the phone.

Is the weather and the exchange rate live?

Yes, when the source answers. Weather is from Open-Meteo and shows the observation time. Rates are from Exchange Rate API, updated on their side about once a day, and marked for reference only. If a source fails, the page says so instead of inventing a number.

How do I get the free eSIM?

Open the eSIM page and tap WhatsApp. You send the message. The offer described here is 5 GB a day for 10 days. Phone compatibility is confirmed in the chat.

Pick the city in the header. The guide, the weather and the rain plan follow that choice.
