GOOGLE SHEET TU DONG - TUY CHON
===============================

He thong Level 2 van hoat dong ngay qua WhatsApp neu chua lam buoc nay.
Cau hinh Google Sheet giup moi don dang ky va booking tu dong luu vao Sheet.

1. Tao mot Google Sheet moi.
2. Mo Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Xoa code cu va dan noi dung file:
   PARTNER_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_Code.gs
4. Bam Deploy > New deployment.
5. Type: Web app.
6. Execute as: Me.
7. Who has access: Anyone.
8. Copy Web app URL.
9. Mo file .env.local trong website va them:

   GOVIETSTAY_PARTNER_WEBHOOK_URL=URL_WEB_APP_CUA_ANH

10. Khoi dong lai: npm run dev

Google Sheet se tu tao 2 tab:
- Applications
- Bookings
